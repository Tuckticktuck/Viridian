Personal data

Settings & rules

Manage backups and review how your system works.

Your data

Tasks and progress are saved to your signed-in Viridian account and cached in this browser. Your account data is protected by database row-level access rules.

Export a backup occasionally. Importing a backup replaces the data currently stored in this browser.

Appearance

Choose an accent palette for your Viridian workspace. The change applies immediately and syncs with your account.

Dashboard layout

Choose which sections appear on your dashboard.

Notifications

Enable browser notifications and set a daily reminder for unfinished tasks. Your browser may ask you to allow notifications.

Permission not requested
Reminders run while Viridian is open. Your browser controls notification permission.

Offline access

Viridian caches its app shell and keeps a local copy of your signed-in tracker. Changes made offline are queued for cloud sync when you reconnect.

Checking connection…
Open Viridian once while online so your Chromebook can cache the app for later.

System rules

  • XP is earned only when you mark a task complete.
  • Recurring tasks appear automatically on their scheduled days.
  • Uncompleted tasks expire at the end of their scheduled day.
  • There is no daily XP cap.
  • Undoing a completion removes that occurrence’s XP.
  • Variable XP starts with a five-level suggestion: 5, 10, 20, 35, or 50 XP. You may adjust it.
  • Level requirements start at 100 XP and rise by 20 XP each level.
  • Task streaks count consecutive scheduled occurrences completed.
  • Overall streaks count consecutive calendar days with at least one completed task.

Privacy note

Sign in with your own account on each device to load the same tracker. Do not share your login details. The GitHub Pages website itself is public, but each account can access only its own tracker row through the database access rules.

Reset tracker

Resetting permanently clears all tasks, completions, and XP from this browser. Export a backup first if you may want to keep your progress.

Cloud data is saved to your Supabase account. Changes are checked for updates on other signed-in devices periodically; keep a backup for extra safety.
